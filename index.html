Who we are

Power solutions built around your load

SetGlobalTech is a renewable energy and power solutions company. We supply and install reliable, affordable solar and inverter systems for homes, businesses, offices, shops, and other facilities.

Every system is customised from a site inspection and load assessment, so the inverter, panels, and battery match how you use power. Tell us your appliances and budget, and we will recommend the right system.
